Customer policies

For help with an order, contact craftguruu@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 88243 50056.

Privacy

We use the contact, delivery and order information you provide to process your order, provide support, and send the updates you request. We do not ask customers to send payment-card details over WhatsApp or email.

If you accept advertising cookies, we use the Meta Pixel to share page visits, product identifiers, cart and checkout actions, and confirmed order values with Meta to measure our ads and personalise advertising on Facebook and Instagram. Meta may associate these actions with your account under its Privacy Policy. Our event code does not send your contact details, delivery address, or personalised order text. Declining advertising cookies does not prevent shopping or checkout.

Shipping

Craftguru currently dispatches orders within India only. Dispatch and delivery timing can vary by product availability, customisation and destination. We will share relevant order or courier updates using the contact details provided with the order.

Terms & conditions

Prices, stock availability, product photographs and product details may change before an order is confirmed. For custom, bulk or corporate orders, the agreed quotation and order confirmation apply.

Purchases & returns

Where a product page states a return window, contact us with your order details within that window so we can review the request. Return eligibility can depend on the item condition and whether it was personalised or made to order.

These customer-policy summaries should be reviewed by Craftguru’s authorised business and legal owner before a production launch.
