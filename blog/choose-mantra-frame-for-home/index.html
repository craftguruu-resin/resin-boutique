How to Choose a Mantra Frame for Your Home

Compare mantra frame wording, size, design and display options to choose a meaningful resin frame for your prayer space, home or a gift.

Read the text and design

Choose a mantra or spiritual image that is meaningful to the recipient. Read the wording and script carefully in the listing, and ask for a clearer view if needed. If you want different text, confirm whether the selected design can be customised before ordering.

Measure the display space

Compare the frame dimensions with the shelf, wall or prayer area where it will be displayed. Check whether the piece stands or hangs and what supports or fittings are included. Leave enough room around it so the artwork and lettering can be seen clearly.

Consider care and gifting

Keep decorative resin away from direct flame and high heat, including nearby lamps or incense holders. Follow the product's care instructions for cleaning. For a gift, confirm the delivery address, packaging requirements and any personal message with the team before the order is finalised.
